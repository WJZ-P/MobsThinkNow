# Zombie profession asset provenance

- `zombie-profession-concepts.png` is the internal visual exploration generated for this project on 2026-08-01.
- `zombie-profession-head-redesign-v2.png` is the second concept pass focused on square Minecraft-native
  head silhouettes, face detail and profession readability.
- `zombie-profession-skin-preview.png` is a deterministic front/back preview rendered from the final 64x64 textures.
- The nine runtime textures under `assets/mobsthinknow/textures/entity/zombie/profession/` are generated by
  `tools/generate_zombie_profession_skins.py` from the matching locally installed Minecraft zombie texture.
- The generator preserves the vanilla UV layout and green zombie anatomy, then paints original profession-specific
  palettes, straps, badges, bracers and boots at native pixel resolution. Both the base 8x8 face and the expanded
  second head layer are used for headbands, scars, open-face helmets, goggles, hoods, visors and chin straps.
- The deterministic preview includes a nearest-neighbour head close-up so accidental smoothing and indistinct
  face treatments can be caught before packaging.
- No texture or logo from another mod is used.

The concept sheet and preview are documentation assets and are not packaged into the production mod JAR.
